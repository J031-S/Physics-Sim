Select: enable Bodies and/or Fields in tool settings, drag a box, then drag a selected item to move the connected assembly. Z toggles snapping. Pause to move an assembly containing locked positions. Pins and world anchors move with the layout. Belt: click two balls. Pulley: click endpoint, wheel ball, endpoint. Cable ends can move at any angle and connect to springs. Pulley axles start pinned; unlock position to move them. Select a belt for crossed / driven modes. Select a spring to lock its current angle.
