# Minitool feedback

One section for each of the 75 minitools, in the order of the catalogue page.

How to fill it in:

- Put an `x` in one box: `[x] Happy` or `[x] Needs changes`. A section left blank means "not looked at yet".
- Under **Changes**, say what to change and where. The headings are only prompts: delete the ones you don't need, or just write freely.
- Anything that applies to every tool goes under "All minitools" at the top.
