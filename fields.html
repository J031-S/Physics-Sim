Keyboard & mouse
Click
With a source chosen in the toolbar, places one and returns to Grab. Otherwise selects the source under the pointer.
Drag
Moves a source. Dragging empty space pans the view.
Ctrl + drag
Rotates a plate or magnet about its centre.
Arrow keys
Nudge the selected source by 0.05 m (0.25 m with snapping).
Delete
Deletes the selected source.
