Presets

Ready-made launches. Loading one removes the satellites in flight, sets the launch values and a suitable time warp, and waits: press Run when you are ready. Each says what to check in the readouts.

Keyboard & mouse
Drag the arrow
Sets the launch speed and direction. Hold Shift to keep the direction and change only the speed.
Drag the launch point
Moves it up or down, changing the launch altitude.
Click a satellite
Selects it for the readouts and graphs.
Drag · scroll
Pan and zoom the view. Either turns off “keep in view”.
L
Launch a satellite
Space · .
Run or pause · advance one frame while paused
[ · ]
Slower · faster time warp
R · C
Reset (remove every satellite) · clear trails
Arrow keys
Up and down change the launch speed by 1% of the circular speed; left and right turn the launch direction by 1°.
S
Switch between Satellites and the Gravity sandbox. In the sandbox: choose a body and press Place, then click the scene (hold Ctrl to place several); drag a body to move it; drag the handle on the selected body's arrow to set its velocity (Shift keeps the direction); Delete removes it; R puts the scene back as it was before Run.
Z
Gravity sandbox: toggle snapping. Positions snap to half a grid square; velocities to 15° steps from the line to the body each one goes round and to 0.05 of the circular speed.
V · G · P · T · K · E
Toggle velocity · gravitational force · predicted orbit · trails · equal areas · equipotentials
A · + · −
Keep the whole orbit in view · zoom in · zoom out
Escape
Closes any open panel

Gravity is Newton's law, F = GMm/r², with G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg². The central body is held fixed, which is accurate while the satellite's mass is negligible beside it. There is no atmosphere, the body does not spin, and nothing else pulls on the satellite.
