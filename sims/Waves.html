Sign convention: real is positive. Object distance u is positive for a real object, image distance v positive for a real image (beyond a lens, in front of a mirror) and negative for a virtual one; f is positive for a converging lens or concave mirror; a surface radius is positive when its centre of curvature is on the side the light leaves by. Then 1/f = 1/u + 1/v and m = −v/u.
