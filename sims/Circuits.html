Keyboard & mouse
Click or drag from the toolbar
Choose a component, then click the grid to place it; or drag it straight from the toolbar. Dropped on a wire, it is put in series.
Drag from a terminal
Draws a wire to wherever you let go. A wire that ends on another wire joins it there. With the Wire tool, start from any grid point.
Click a component
Selects it: its values, current, p.d. and power appear on the right, and its graph is drawn. Clicking a switch also opens or closes it.
Drag a component
Moves it; the wires on its terminals follow.
R · Delete
Turn the selected component a quarter-turn · delete it

Every component is ideal. Wires, ammeters and closed switches have no resistance at all; voltmeters and open switches pass no current at all. A cell is an EMF with an optional internal resistance. In the AC view the meters read RMS values over the last whole cycle, and the graphs are an oscilloscope showing the last three cycles. A lamp is a fixed resistance, whatever its brightness. Current is conventional (from + round to −) unless Electron flow is on.
